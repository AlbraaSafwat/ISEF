Phase 1: Proof-of-Concept Prototype PRELIMINARY DISPLAY

Because post-harvest rice straw is seasonal during the preliminary exhibition window, a physical lignocellulosic mock-up validates manufacturing feasibility:

  • Matrix & Dimensions: Coarse lignocellulosic wood sawdust pressed into a 13 × 8 × 2 cm panel.
  • Exact Bio-Shield Formulation: 30 wt% gelatinized cornstarch binder + 10 wt% dissolved Potassium Alum.
  • Thermal Curing Validation: Controlled ventilated oven dehydration at 60–70 °C in 45-minute flip cycles to prevent warping and verify structural integrity.
Phase 2: Laboratory Execution Plan (Post-SRC) STEM OCT2 LAB

Full experimental validation at the STEM OCT2 11th District school laboratory under Adult Sponsor Safaa Ezzat:

  • Sample Matrix: Fabricate 12 milled rice-straw specimens (4 Alum ratios: 0%, 5%, 10%, 15% × n = 3 replicates) plus 3 commercial EPS controls.
  • Standardized Testing: Adapted ASTM C518 guarded hot-box thermal testing, fume-hood UL-94 15 s flame tests, and 24-hour ASTM D570 water absorption.
  • Statistical & Durability Upgrades: Evaluate significance via One-Way ANOVA (α = 0.05), IR thermography, and apply a natural beeswax moisture barrier.
